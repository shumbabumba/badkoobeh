### Weather Manager
The **Weather Manager** is responsible for retrieving, processing, storing, and providing wind data that determines how text fragments move across the map. Instead of making separate weather requests for individual fragments, it creates a shared geographic grid covering the map's territory, with requested sample points spaced approximately 9 km apart. The grid is extended beyond the map boundaries to ensure that nearby weather samples are available even for fragments close to the edges. During startup, the manager sends the grid coordinates to the [Open-Meteo ECMWF API](https://open-meteo.com/), requesting hourly wind speed and direction at 10 metres above ground. The request includes the previous hour and the next 48 hours, uses metres per second as the wind speed unit, and specifies UTC to keep timestamps consistent regardless of the visitor's local timezone. Once the response is received, the manager processes the returned weather data, identifies unique model grid cells, and converts wind speed and direction into eastward and northward vector components. These samples, together with their hourly timestamps, are stored in memory and shared by all text fragments. To estimate wind at a particular geographic location, the `getWindAt()` method identifies up to four nearest valid weather samples within the calculated interpolation coverage distance and combines their wind vectors using inverse-square distance weighting, giving closer samples greater influence. It also interpolates between consecutive hourly values to estimate wind at the requested timestamp. If a fragment is located outside the supported grid, or no valid weather data is available, the method returns `null` rather than extrapolating unsupported values. The manager also prevents duplicate simultaneous requests by sharing an ongoing fetch and reusing the initial startup promise. The downloaded weather grid remains cached for the duration of the session, allowing fragments to obtain geographically and temporally interpolated wind estimates without repeatedly contacting the API.
